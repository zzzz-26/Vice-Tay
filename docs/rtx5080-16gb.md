# An RTX 5080 with eight cores: `-Machine 4070tisuper`

On the PC below the same scene ran at a median of 16 fps with the RTX 4090 settings and 27 fps with `-Machine 4070tisuper`, the preset for a 16 GB card and eight cores. `-Machine auto` now gives an NVIDIA card of 10 to 19 GB those memory settings itself, and up to eight cores the presets' three pipeline workers: 27.5 fps here (run 12). The presets are unchanged, and `-Machine 4090` still gives the 4090 settings to any card.

When the nine runs below were made `-Machine auto` gave every NVIDIA card all of the RTX 4090 settings, and that is what "auto" means in their table. From `7afb2da` it sized three worker counts to the CPU (8 / 6 / 4 on eight cores) and kept the 4090's memory settings; [later runs](#the-automatic-profile-since-7afb2da) put that at 16.4 fps against the preset's 28.9.

| Part | |
|---|---|
| CPU | AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D (8 cores of one kind, 16 threads, one cache) |
| GPU | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5080 16 GB, driver 617.42, DXGI budget 15,209 MB, Resizable BAR on (`card memory the CPU maps supported=true`) |
| RAM | 2 × 32 GB DDR5-6000, a system-managed page file |
| Display | 3840 × 2160 at 60 Hz |
| OS | Windows 11 Pro, build 26300 |

## Why not the default

With the 4090 settings the log reads `card buffer limits vram_budget_mb=15209 card_buffers_min_kb=4096 card_buffers_max_mb=2048`: on a card under 20 GiB only title buffers of 4 MiB or more get a copy in the card's memory. The rest stay in the PC's memory, where every load, store and atomic a shader does on them crosses the bus. The frame line's "on the GPU" part was 22 ms a frame with them and under 1 ms with the preset, which lowers that minimum to 64 KiB.

The 4090 thread counts (12 list translation, 8 memory update, 6 landing, page mapping on several threads, 10 pipeline workers) are for sixteen cores. On eight, the first seconds of the world ran at 3 to 6 fps while 110 to 130 host pipelines were still being made; with the preset's counts none were left by then.

## Measurements

Diagnostics build of `2776268` (`cargo build --locked --profile diag -p victory --features diagnostics`), Series S, full screen, default output, shaders translated ahead with `victory shaders`. Every run started from the same copy of a warmed cache (translated shaders, host pipeline library, early pipeline list) and of the title's saves, and ran unattended: Enter every 5 s until the world, then nothing for 150 s, which is the opening cutscene (about 3,100 draws a frame). Every run was recorded from the screen at 30 fps on the card's encoder.

Frame columns are over the frame reports in the world (one about every 5 s, 1,000 draws or more). Memory is the peak of samples taken every 10 s.

What each part of the preset does here:

- **Buffers in the card's memory** (`VICTORY_CARD_BUFFERS_MIN_KB=64` and the budgets with it): nearly all of the gain. Run 5 keeps them and brings back the 4090 thread counts, and stays at 25.6 fps.
- **The thread counts** (8 / 6 / 4, one page-mapping thread, 3 pipeline workers): 1.6 fps in the median, and the start of the world. With the 4090 counts 110 to 130 pipelines were still being made when the world began (runs 1, 3 and 5). Two shorter runs changed one count each on top of the preset, `VICTORY_PIPELINE_WORKERS=10` and `VICTORY_MAP_THREADS=auto`: neither left a pipeline unmade at the world's start. It takes the counts together.
- **Windows in card memory the CPU maps** (`VICTORY_WINDOWS_BAR_MB=4096`): 0.5 fps, within what two identical runs differ by, and 1.7 GB less private memory for 1 GB more of the card's.
- `VICTORY_READBACK_POOL=1` changed nothing here. `VICTORY_HELPER_PRIORITY=1` ran 0.5 fps higher in one run; that is one run.

The frame clock's target is 33.3 ms. The release build with mimalloc reaches it in this scene (run 9); the diagnostics build does not. Run 9 changes the build and the allocator together, and its frame line has no "on the GPU" part. This is the opening cutscene only: the open world draws more (4,400 draws a frame and up in [measurements.md](measurements.md)) and was not measured here.

## The automatic profile since `7afb2da`

Diagnostics build of `fcded4e`, the same method, one copy of a warmed cache for both runs (the translator had not changed: `victory shaders` made all 25,788 translations again and none came out different).

| Run | Settings | fps median / p10 | ms a frame | preparing | on the GPU | copying back | Private memory | Card memory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | `-Machine 4070tisuper` | **28.9** / 23.7 | 34.6 | 26.4 | 0.8 | 12.2 | 25.2 GB | 13.4 GB |
| 11 | `-Machine auto` (8 / 6 / 4, the 4090 memory settings) | 16.4 / 3.5 | 60.9 | 27.0 | 21.7 | 16.6 | 27.7 GB | 12.3 GB |

The worker counts the automatic profile sized in run 11 are not what held it back: its log still read `card_buffers_min_kb=4096`, and the "on the GPU" part is the 22 ms of runs 1 and 3. It also kept 10 pipeline workers next to `VICTORY_MAP_THREADS=auto`: the world began with 125 pipelines still being made (run 10: none) and three reports under 10 fps.

Run 12 is the automatic profile as `scripts\play.ps1` now makes it for this PC (a 16 GB card, eight cores), on the same build and the same copy of the cache as runs 10 and 11: the preset's memory settings (`card_buffers_min_kb=64`, windows in card memory the CPU maps) and 3 pipeline workers, with `VICTORY_MAP_THREADS=auto` left as the automatic profile had it. No pipeline was left to make when the world began. It is one run; the 1.4 fps to run 10, whose only other difference is one page-mapping thread, is more than two identical runs differed by here (0.1 and 0.6) and was not run again.

## In play: fills behind placed copies

The runs above are the opening cutscene. In the prison, the first part the player controls (4,000 to 6,800 draws a frame), the submitting thread waited 5 to 10 ms a frame for flights: most frames one submission was refused by the steps done on the card and waited.

That wait is new with `df01b38` (#344), found by building the commits between two runs of the same preset from one copy of the cache, 75 s of the opening cutscene each:

| Commit | Fills done on the card a frame | Submissions refused and waited a frame | Waiting for flights |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a33271f` (before #344) | 1,564 | 0.00 | 0.7 ms |
| `54bbb7f` | 1,700 | 0.00 | 0.7 ms |
| `df01b38` | 641 | 0.30 | 1.9 ms |
| `10675b5` (#344 merged) | 622 | 0.29 | 1.6 ms |
| `aa917f3` | 672 | 0.26 | 1.8 ms |
| `aa917f3` with `VICTORY_PLACED_FILL_REDIRECT=1` | 1,045 | 0.06 | 0.9 ms |

`df01b38` makes a submission wait when a read left on the CPU depends on a copy at its place whose bytes a flight still writes, which is right, and lets a fill read that copy's source on the card instead. The second part is `VICTORY_PLACED_FILL_REDIRECT=1`, off by default, so the default has the waits without what avoids most of them.

In play the switch was changed every 4 or 5 s inside one run (`VICTORY_AB`), three runs of the same prison section played by hand from the prologue's save, diagnostics build of `1d83085` with `-Machine auto`. Phases of 3,000 draws a frame or more:

| Run | Other switch changing with it | Frame time with the redirect, mean of full cycles | Cycles faster | Refused and waited a frame, off / on | Fills on the card a frame, off / on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | – | −1.5 ms (± 0.8) | 12 of 16 | 0.71 / 0.12 | 101 / 1,577 |
| 14 | `VICTORY_PORT_JOURNAL` | −2.5 ms (± 2.1) | 10 of 13 | 0.68 / 0.13 | 89 / 1,909 |
| 15 | `VICTORY_HELPER_PRIORITY` | −2.8 ms (± 2.2) | 9 of 13 | 0.67 / 0.14 | 88 / 1,706 |

Weighted by their errors the three give −1.7 ms (± 0.7) on frames of 37 to 49 ms; 31 of 42 cycles were faster. The frame gains less than the wait it loses, since part of that wait is for work of the card the frame needs anyway. No run had an error or lost the device. `scripts\play.ps1` now sets the switch for NVIDIA cards outside `-Safe`, where it sets `VICTORY_PLACED_ORDER=1`.

Runs 14 and 15 do not settle their other switch: `VICTORY_PORT_JOURNAL` −3.0 ms (± 2.3), 8 of 13 cycles faster; `VICTORY_HELPER_PRIORITY` −1.4 ms (± 3.1), 7 of 13.

## What it looks like

Each recording was reviewed from one frame every 5 s, and runs 2 and 3 side by side at the same shot at full size. With the preset the picture is the same as with the 4090 settings: the same shots, credits and HUD, the same lighting. Under every setting, coloured noise on one character's shorts in the first shot of the pair.

One artifact, in runs 5 and 11 only: at the start of the world, while the frame rate was at 3 to 5 fps and pipelines were still being made, the black bars above and below the picture were pale green (run 5: about 6 s, 0:39 to 0:45 of its recording, with 401 draws a frame dropped; run 11: about 12 s, 0:41 to 0:53). Both runs had 10 pipeline workers and page mapping on several threads. Runs 10 to 12 were reviewed the same way as the others; run 12 shows the same shots as run 10 and no bars.

Runs 13 to 15 were recorded and reviewed from one frame every 7 to 10 s: the prison, the walk with the guard, the common room, the office and the scenes after it look the same in the three, with nothing that comes and goes with the switch's phases at that sampling. In all three the robbery scene that follows has very bright highlights; that was not compared with the switch held off for a whole run.

## Memory

At 150 s into the world the game's private memory was 25 to 28 GB and the PC had 18 to 21 GB left to commit of 67.6 GB (64 GB of RAM and a 4 GB page file). Below 12 GiB of free RAM or of room left to commit, the layer keeps its idle caches in the PC's memory (staging buffers, windows) for 10 s instead of 300 s. A longer session was not run; a larger page file keeps that further away.
